\subsection{Training must acquire useful slopes and centers}
\label{sec:feature-acquisition}

Joint training also leaves a substantial precision gap over this budget.
After five million updates, joint Adam reaches median dense-grid relative
error $1.81\times10^{-3}$, versus $6.62\times10^{-7}$ on the supplied
$\lambda=1/4$ features (Figure~\ref{fig:note-training}, left).

The population's mean bandwidth remains below the supplied reference
(Figure~\ref{fig:note-training}, right). Refitting the readout on the learned
features barely improves their error (Appendix~\ref{app:note-evaluation}),
so unfinished readout optimization alone does not explain the gap.
The next analysis therefore concerns the joint acquisition of useful slopes
and centers. The construction supplies both this geometry and the readout
directly.
