## What was actually achieved

**1. A step-2 solver that reaches machine epsilon on a frozen $\Phi$ at $O(d)$ state.** Block-QR whitening (contiguous pivoted-Householder blocks, $k{=}128$) + LSMR hits geo-mean $4.1\times10^{-14}$ across 9 cells, at or below the truncated-SVD floor, where plain CGLS stalls at $10^{-6}$. Hardened over 6 targets $\times$ 4 widths, noise (lands on the $0.27\sigma$ statistical floor), batching ($b\ge4d$), and fp32 (drives to fp32's floor). The full recipe with every do/don't: `results/_archive/checkpoint_D_optimizers/expD09_2nd_order_regime/expD09_recipe_results.md` (fig `figures/blockqr_k128.png`); hardening ledger: `expD10_step2_hardening/expD10_hardening_results.md`. The control rules ($\alpha$ parameterization, $\text{test}_2\le\alpha$ stopping, the damping floor $\alpha\ge r_{\text{entry}}$ whose absence lets observable and true error diverge by up to $19{,}849\times$, the $0.9r$-iteration terminal solve) are all in **`results/_archive/checkpoint_D_optimizers/expD12_mu_ladder/STEP2_SOLVER_SPEC.md`** — the single most reusable document of the campaign, and the only writeup of expD12/expD13.

**2. Machine epsilon banked in-run from QI init, 1-D and 2-D — the $10^{-15}$/$10^{-16}$ result.** The final assembled optimizer (expD14 iteration 4, the `latch`: one held-out signal $s$ read at init selects learn mode = uncooled $r_{\text{entry}}$-damped solve + free Adam, or preserve mode = geometry gated by $\min(1,s/s_{\text{ref}})$ + solve at $\alpha{=}s$) reaches $2.5\times10^{-16}$/$1.5\times10^{-15}$/$1.3\times10^{-16}$ on the 1-D `qi` targets with floors intact, and $1.5\times10^{-14}$ on the 2-D radon geometry at $N{=}576$ where Adam degrades the same init to $6.8\times10^{-3}$. Floors are held-out-validated (the raw min-norm finisher was wrong in 26/132 cells). `results/_archive/checkpoint_D_optimizers/expD14_lobotomy/iteration_4/iteration_4_results.md`, figs `suite/figures/S1_scaling.png`, `S2_losscurves.png`; code `experiments/_archive/expD14_lobotomy/iteration_4/core4.py`. The earlier all-18-cells-at-$10^{-16}$ table (iteration 0, `iteration_0/figures/T4_grid_qi.png`) is real but cost-dishonest: that arm was the dense reference solver at 572 passes/step ($143\times$ Adam) with the probe never counted — read iteration 0's correction header before citing it.

**3. From Xavier random init, the solve-in-training beats Adam-plus-finisher — but only to $\sim10^{-5}$-$10^{-7}$, not the floor.** The uncooled $\alpha_t=r_{\text{entry}}$ arm leaves a `rand`/`sine_8pi` geometry worth $7.5\times10^{-7}$: $\sim200\times$ better than Adam+terminal-solve and $6\times10^5\times$ better than init+solve (iteration 2, `iteration_2/figures/G1_scorecard.png`). Mechanistically settled on the way: solving $L$ exactly in-training stuns feature learning *intrinsically* (not a throttle artifact — cleared in iteration 1 with all throttles off; coherent travel collapses $1.0\to0.03$, geometry SNR reads 0, $\|v\|$ inflates to $10^4$), while under-solving ($\tau{=}1$ LSQR) keeps learning alive and beats Adam's own geometry $4$-$10\times$ at $2\times$ cost (`iteration_1/figures/F1_floor_trajectories.png`, `F3_diagnostics.png`).

**4. The 2-layer / real-data results, located.** Two distinct things: (a) expD07's `dl_test` — fp64 2-hidden-layer MLPs on three real regression tasks — where the two-stage **`padam_hb`** (Adam warmup 30% $\to$ freeze $D=\sqrt{\hat v}$ $\to$ preconditioned heavy ball with trust window) was **best or within noise of best on all three tasks**, e.g. parkinsons train MSE $1.4\times10^{-2}$ vs Adam's $3.2\times10^{-2}$ (`results/_archive/checkpoint_D_optimizers/expD07_lstsq_optim_suite/dl_test/dl_curves.png`, code `dl_test/run.py`). (b) iteration 11's depth-2 bench from random init: certificate correctly discovers $(v,c_0)$ (65 of 4353 params, refusing $W_2,b_2$) and reaches $5.6\times10^{-11}$ on `sine` where Adam gets $4.5\times10^{-3}$ — but *loses* on `runge` (readout-pinning damaged feature learning $3$ orders), which is the finding that drove iterations 1-4. The final `latch` passes the dl litmus at $1.2$-$2.6\times$ Adam's train MSE without winning it.

**5. The hard negative: no $O(d\cdot k)$ iterative solver reaches the fp64 floor on QI spectra.** Reaching the floor needs stored orthogonality $c\approx r$, i.e. $\Theta(d^2)$; the binding quantity is the number of distinct singular-value scales (QI spectra are gapless, median ratio 1.03; a 2-level spectrum solves at $c{=}0$), and every preconditioner tested caps at $\sim10^{-6}$. This is what forces the tiered design (cheap recurring solve + one finisher) and it kills a large design space permanently. `results/_archive/checkpoint_D_optimizers/expD11_batching/expD11_results.md` + `lead_B_results.md`, figs `figures/D1_two_regimes.png`, `D2_spectral_law.png`, `D3_scaling_verdict.png`.

**6. Discovery of the linear set $L$, solved.** The exact-zero fixed-point rule ($L$ = the set whose Jacobian columns do not move under a probe step supported on $L$; bitwise zeros, survives any dtype) holds **100% precision on every architecture tested** (skip, fixed basis, depth-2, ResNet, conv, transformer), refuses attention/layer-norm/earlier blocks, and amortizes to $\sim$1 pass per step via a per-parameter Beta belief. Supersedes iteration 11's $2(2m{+}1)$-forward curvature probe outright. **`results/_archive/checkpoint_D_optimizers/expD15_inclusion_score/METHOD_L_selection.md`** (the four mechanisms + costs) and `expD15_results.md` (the purity-is-everything measurement: +2% wrong params costs $5\times$, +5% four orders).

**7. Two laws worth keeping in your head.** The coupling law: one Adam step after an exact solve re-injects $\|v\|\eta$ of error (measured over five decades of $\|v\|$, six of $\eta$; `results/_archive/checkpoint_D_optimizers/expD08_qi_init_nlcg/iteration_11/coupling_law.png`) — min-norm solves are protective because they keep $\|v\|$ small. And the single-solve accuracy law: a damped solve lands at eval error $\approx0.3\alpha$ over ten decades, so $\alpha$ *is* the accuracy you choose to bank (iteration 0, T1).

## What failed

- **Drift-matched (two-sided) $\mu$**: 4 orders worse than fixed low $\alpha$, caused weight blowup $\|v\|\ 0.55\to402$. Kill list, `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` §5.
- **Trust-region clip on the $L$ step**: costs 5-8 orders (iteration 0 T2).
- **Any control comparing loss values**: unresolvable in fp64 below $\sim10^{-12}$; watch gradient norms.
- **The dense curvature probe / iteration 11's certificate**: $2(2m{+}1)$ forwards, never counted, and its $10^{-3}$ relative step rounds away in bf16. Superseded by expD15.
- **expD14 iteration 0's cost table**: headline arm understated $82\times$; "precision-agnostic" claim false (9 hardcoded fp64 constants). Corrections header on the file.
- **Preset $\alpha$ schedules**: diverge up to $10^7$ across inits (iteration 2); the signal must be measured.
- **Train-side expressibility cooling** (`perp`): freezes stock-random learning — train fit conflates "features right" with "features overfit" (iteration 3).
- **Propose-and-verify discovery at $O(\log P)$**: collapses at 2-D ranking purity; the fixed-point method replaced it (expD15).

## Still promising, if time permits

- **The one real gap-closer: a geometry *refiner*.** No mechanism improves an already-decent geometry toward construction quality — Adam only damages it, and `rand_scale` floors fall only $\sim N^{-2.4}$ with width (iteration 4, open questions). This is the single item standing between the latch and the success criterion.
- **The mode threshold**: `rand`/`runge` sits on the learn/preserve boundary and freezes; $s_{\text{ref}}=10^{-3}$ classifies every measured cell but needs confirmation on fresh cases (or hysteresis).
- **dl parity**: let Adam keep stepping $L$ in learn mode (`adam_on_L`, measured neutral-to-positive) so the solve supplements rather than displaces Adam on noisy data.
- **`datagap` blindness**: no signal sees geometry damage where there is no data; a per-unit coverage guard from already-computed column norms is the sketched fix (iteration 2).
- **The 2-D learn-mode cases at expE01 scale**, and width scaling of the random-init result — both untouched.

## Remaining gaps

The success criterion — machine epsilon with $O(\log(1/\varepsilon))$ width scaling from a *non-construction* init — is not met. What exists: preservation and banking of a correct geometry to $10^{-16}$/$10^{-14}$ (1-D/2-D), and learning from random init to $10^{-5}$-$10^{-7}$. The missing piece is the refinement regime between them: once a geometry is good enough that the solve engages, nothing currently improves it further, so runs bank whatever floor their init family allows and stop descending.
